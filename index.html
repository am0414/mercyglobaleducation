02 Our Story

It began with parents asking how their children could keep learning in Japanese while living abroad.

A place to learn that doesn't depend
on where you live.

Outside Japan, options for Japanese-language education are limited, and many families never find teaching they can rely on. In 2020 we began as a single online school.

Requests followed — for subjects beyond Japanese, and for one-on-one attention rather than a group. We built services to meet them. Today families in 34 countries choose between three different ways of learning with us.

04 Milestones

Milestones

2016

Mercy G.K. founded in Japan

Started as a neighborhood tutoring school for elementary and middle school students.

2020

Success Academy launched

Started as an online school for children with Japanese roots living outside Japan.

2021

Curriculum expanded

Beyond language arts and math, classes were added to widen what children take an interest in.

2022

English-taught English classes introduced

Added to help children returning to Japan hold on to the English they had built abroad.

2023

Students in more than 15 countries

Classrooms grew to hold children from an increasingly wide range of backgrounds.

2025

Mercy Global Education LLC established

A U.S. entity was set up in Florida to serve families in the United States.

2026

BrightBe Learning and BrightBe One launch

Group lessons for U.S. families and one-on-one tutoring joined the company, bringing it to three services.
